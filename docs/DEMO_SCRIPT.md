# 2-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:15 — the problem

“Most campus maps give everyone the same fastest route. But a flooded ramp, poor lighting, or a crowded courtyard can turn that route into a dead end. Wayfinder treats access as a route requirement, not an afterthought.”

## 0:15–0:40 — personalized plan

“Amina is leaving the Library for the Wellness Center in the rain. She selects Step-free and rain conditions. Wayfinder chooses the covered Rain Garden ramp and shows an 8-minute, 540-metre plan.”

Click **Generate my route** and point to the dotted teal route.

## 0:40–1:05 — adaptability

“Now it is dark. Add High visibility and Night. The routing policy favors verified, lit connections. This is not a chatbot guessing at directions: it is a local graph algorithm with clear infrastructure attributes.”

Turn on **High visibility** and **Night**.

## 1:05–1:30 — community response

“A student reports that the curb ramp is flooded. That one small signal should immediately restore someone else’s independence.”

Click **Report a barrier**, submit, then point to the alert and updated route.

## 1:30–1:50 — transparent trust

“The most important screen is ‘Why this route?’ Wayfinder explains the trade-offs: step-free access, cover, lighting, and the community report. No one is asked to share a diagnosis to get a route that works.”

Click **Why this route?**.

## 1:50–2:00 — close

“Wayfinder can scale as a PWA using verified campus GIS data, but it begins with a simple premise: independence is infrastructure.”
